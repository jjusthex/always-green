Always Green

Speed advice for catching green lights, learned from your own drives.

Before your first drive

  • Advice only. Always obey the actual light, signs and traffic around you. The app can be wrong, especially early on.
  • It learns as you drive. New lights say "Learning". Early advice is labeled Guess, then Likely, then Sure.
  • It never advises speeding. Where the speed limit isn't in the map data, it only ever suggests slowing down.
  • Hands off the phone. Mount it, tap Start once before you move, and listen. Michigan law allows only a single touch while driving.
  • Keep the app open with the screen on while driving. iPhone pauses location for web apps in the background.
